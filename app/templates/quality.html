Our Quality Policy

Quality Built into Every Step of the Formulation Lifecycle

At RP PHARMA, quality is not merely a final inspection step; it is systematically engineered into formulation design, raw material procurement, manufacturing processes, and final packaging.

Our formulations are manufactured through state-of-the-art facilities across India that adhere to applicable WHO-GMP and EU-GMP standards and requirements. We maintain complete batch traceability and analytical transparency to ensure healthcare products meet the highest safety, efficacy, and regulatory criteria.

Pharmacopeial Compliance

Products formulated to IP, BP, USP, and international monographs.

Batch Consistency

Stringent in-process controls verifying content uniformity and dissolution.

Quality Architecture

Quality Control (QC)
HPLC, GC, dissolution profiling, and microbiological assays.
Quality Assurance (QA)
SOP enforcement, change control, validation, and batch release protocols.
Traceability & Integrity
Comprehensive batch manufacturing records (BMR) and audit trails.
Regulatory Support

Comprehensive Technical Documentation

We support our global distribution partners with end-to-end regulatory filings tailored to international health authorities.

Quality Assurance

Commitment to consistent product quality, certified manufacturing facilities, and comprehensive QA oversight across all production partners.

Regulatory Documentation

Technical and regulatory documentation supporting products, including CTD / ACTD format dossiers, Certificates of Pharmaceutical Product (COPP), and Free Sale Certificates.

Stability Studies

Stability studies and supporting data where applicable, conducted under ICH Zone IVb conditions (30°C / 75% RH & 40°C / 75% RH) for tropical climate stability.

Process Validation

Validated processes and analytical documentation where applicable, verifying repeatability, cleaning validation, and analytical method verification.
