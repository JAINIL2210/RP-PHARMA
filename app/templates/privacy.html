Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

RP PHARMA ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the business and personal information you provide when using our corporate website and B2B communication channels.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you explicitly submit through our contact forms, product inquiry forms, and business partnership applications, including:

  • Contact information (Full Name, Official Email Address, Phone/WhatsApp Number, Country).
  • Company information (Business Name, Business Type, Operating Markets).
  • Product specifications, estimated volumes, and attached registration or company documents.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used exclusively for:

  • Responding to your product inquiries and commercial quotation requests.
  • Evaluating distribution rights, dossier licensing, and contract manufacturing proposals.
  • Complying with applicable export, regulatory, and legal verification standards.

4. Information Confidentiality & Security

RP PHARMA does not sell, lease, or share business inquiry data with third-party marketing entities. Information is accessed solely by authorized regulatory, commercial, and technical personnel.
